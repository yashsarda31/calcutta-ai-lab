// questions

What people usually ask.

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp. A person replies, not a bot.

Do you give tips or tell me what to buy?

No. We build tools that run the rules you give us: scanners, backtests, journals and reports. Every trading decision stays yours. We are not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or research analyst.

Will automation make me profitable?

Nobody can promise that, and we don't. Often the most useful result is a backtest showing that an idea doesn't work after costs, before you put money on it.

Can it place orders for me?

By default we send alerts and you place the trade. Order automation is possible only through your broker's official API, on your own account, within your broker's algo rules and with hard limits you set. We check those rules first.

Which brokers and data do you work with?

Indian brokers with an official API (Zerodha Kite Connect, for example), TradingView, NSE and BSE data, Excel and Google Sheets. We confirm what fits your setup on the first visit.

Do you need my trading password?

No. Scanners, backtests and journals don't need access to your trading account at all. For order automation you connect your own API key, and it stays under your control.

How much does it cost?

A one-time setup fee and a fixed monthly fee, quoted in writing after the free visit and before any work starts. The price depends on the job, so we quote once we've seen it.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. If you can use Telegram or WhatsApp, you can use this. We set everything up and look after it.

Do you also automate business work?

Yes: WhatsApp orders into Tally, payment reminders and a daily sales report, for wholesalers, shops, manufacturers, clinics and CA firms.
