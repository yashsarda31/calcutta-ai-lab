Questions

What owners usually ask.

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp. A person replies, not a bot.

Do my customers need to change how they order?

No. They keep messaging your WhatsApp number exactly as they do now. The change happens on your side.

Does it work with Tally?

Yes. Entries can go into Tally, or into Excel or Google Sheets if that's what you use. We check your setup on the first visit.

What if the AI reads an order wrong?

Anything it isn't sure about is sent to you or your staff to confirm before it's entered. During setup we run it alongside your team until the entries match.

How much does it cost?

A one-time setup fee and a fixed monthly fee, quoted in writing after the free visit and before any work starts. The price depends on the job, so we quote after we've seen it.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use this. We set everything up and look after it.

Is my business data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts: your Tally, your sheets, your WhatsApp. We only access what the job needs, and we never sell or share it.

Do you come to our office?

Yes, anywhere in Kolkata. Or visit us at 229 AJC Bose Road, Monday to Saturday, 10 AM–6 PM.
